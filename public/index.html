Get to Know Your Farmer

Amie Neff

Hopyard Farm is owned by Amie Neff and Doug Eck, who started out in Indiana and made their way to California with their kids Sam and Olivia, almost 20 years ago. There were several stops along the way in Albuquerque where Amie earned an MS in Architecture (go Lobos!), Lugano Switzerland, and Montreal Canada. Doug repairs the occasional broken equipment, but Amie has always wanted to grow food, and is living out her dream on the farm in Graton, Sonoma County. We grow organic vegetables, heirloom melons, cut flowers, and orchard fruit using regenerative practices that build living soil and nurture native habitat.

Every Thursday afternoon, Amie opens the Barnstand at 2590 Edison Street to share the week's harvest with neighbors walking the West County Regional Trail or stopping by from town.
